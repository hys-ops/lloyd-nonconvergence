Exact Lloyd iteration · Every dimension n ≥ 3

Nonconvergence of two-site population Lloyd iteration

Even for the uniform law on a smooth, positively curved convex body arbitrarily close to a ball, the exact two-site population Lloyd iteration need not converge to a single configuration.

One orbit, a continuum of stationary limits

Cluster set ≅ ℝPn−2 (n ≥ 3)

From a suitable antipodal start, the unordered pairs accumulate at a common radius on every equatorial axis. The two sites stay distinct. Ordinary and optimal equal-mass updates coincide along that orbit.

Proof and research path

From an exact hemisphere update to an orbit

Central symmetry preserves antipodality and gives each of the two Voronoi cells half the mass. The ordinary nearest-site assignment therefore also satisfies the equal-mass constraint. The proof uses the exact update in this setting, not a bound on an approximate step.

  1. Inspiration, not a proof dependency

    Start with the first moment of a transport cell

    A finite cell-moment comparison drawn from the Brenier stability proof suggested asking what a Lloyd step does to a cell moment. That comparison motivates the question; the orbit proof does not invoke it.

  2. Exact update

    Encode the hemisphere moment by direction

    For an even law set H(u) = E|u · X|. The positive site after the update is H(u)u + ∇SH(u). The spherical derivative controls its change of direction.

  3. Steering

    Prescribe a nonconvergent directional trajectory

    Choose successive directions with positive inner product and prescribe the spherical gradient of log H at each step. The directions approach every equatorial axis.

  4. Realization

    Make the directional function come from a law

    Realize the required angular moment with a suitable law, in particular with the uniform law of a centrally symmetric smooth positive-curvature body close to a ball.

  5. Conclusion

    Prevent collapse without forcing a single limit

    The paired centers remain separated and every accumulation configuration is stationary, but the entire sequence does not converge to one unordered pair.

Formal verification

What Lean checks

The central declaration LloydNDVerified.projective_lloyd verifies the main mathematical existence result by a related formal construction. The public replay packet supplies the source, pinned toolchain and dependencies, verification scripts and archived logs.

The Lean construction is not a line-by-line reproduction of the written proof. Literature comparisons and historical priority are outside the formal verification.

Literature

Related results and sources

A foundational follow-up proved convergence toward the set of nondegenerate fixed configurations under broad assumptions. More recent work proves convergence to a single configuration under a definability assumption. The example lies outside that assumption; it distinguishes convergence to a set from convergence to a point.
